Jendy - Photosomatic generator v5

Big words. Tiny photos. Entirely your memories.

Turn a folder of photos into a photo mosaic: fill your words with real pictures, or create a tiled photo background with words over it. Customise the layout, colours and image size, then download a PNG or JPG.

Your photos stay yours.Photos you choose remain local on your device. Nothing from your photos is uploaded to a server; processing happens in your browser. Your original files are never changed.This app downloads a HEIC/HEIF decoding helper from a public CDN. That helper processes photos locally; an internet connection may be needed to load it.

Start here
Write your words → choose your photos → render → export your image.
Hover over a control, or tap or focus its ? for help.

1. Text & layout

Write your message and shape each line. The layout preview updates as you adjust the lettering.

Each line can be bold, italic or underlined independently. Width % controls the line width; tile size controls photo size.

2. Output image

Choose how the words and photos fit together, then set your canvas size, background and export resolution.

Lower means smaller photos and more tiles. In standard mode, changing this updates every text line; you can still fine-tune individual lines above.
In inverse mode, photos cover the full canvas. The words are either a solid colour or genuinely transparent in the exported PNG. An optional coloured border can outline either style.
100%
100% keeps the full pixel dimensions. Lowering this reduces pixels, file size and render load, but also lowers effective print DPI.
Normal render mode: layout preview and rendered output preview stay available.

3. Photos

Pick a photo folder and control cropping, repetition and placement. More photos give the mosaic more variety.

Chrome or Edge recommended. Unsupported/corrupt files are skipped automatically instead of killing the render.

4. Colour treatment

Give your mosaic a consistent look with original colours, black and white or a tint. These edits only affect the output.

5. Generate

Build the full mosaic, watch its progress and pause or cancel if needed. Export your PNG or JPG when it is ready.
